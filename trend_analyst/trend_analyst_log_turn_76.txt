The **menu spread** shows a *large* number of high‑prestige, high‑price dishes (many 600‑1000 cr) **and** a very dense field of cheap “starter” dishes (10‑30 cr).  Competitors are actively bidding for both scarce high‑value ingredients (see “Top 20 ingredients” below) **and** bulk cheap ones.  

**Trend:** **Both** – the market is split between a premium race for prestige dishes and a volume race for cheap, fast‑turnover meals.  The safest play is to keep a foothold in each segment.
